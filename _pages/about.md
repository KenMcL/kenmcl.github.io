I am a professor of mathematics at Tulane University, where I hold the Evelyn and John G. Phillips Distinguished Chair in Mathematics. I study integrable systems and use the asymptotic analysis of Riemann–Hilbert problems to investigate random matrices and nonlinear waves.

I grew up in Tucson, Arizona, and earned my B.A. and Ph.D. in mathematics at New York University. Before joining Tulane, I led the mathematics departments at the University of Arizona and Colorado State University.
